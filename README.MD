## Notes

Make sure the XRay client is properly installed and configured before use.
For security and stability, never share sensitive information such as your UUID or private key with anyone.

## How to Find Suitable SERVERNAMES and DEST

You can simply use the defaults provided by the docker container.
You can also look for your own SERVERNAMES and DEST — see [Reality - TLS - Scanner](https://github.com/XTLS/RealiTLScanner) and the [known-good list](reality/SERVERNAMES.MD).
